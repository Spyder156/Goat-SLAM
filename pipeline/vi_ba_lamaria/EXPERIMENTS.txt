Full Short refinement experiment contract
=========================================

Sequence: sequence_1_19, full native recording, not a time crop.
Reference: lamaria_radial_fixed_short_full_20261003_short_full, Score2D 67.188379.
Suite: experiments/lamaria_global_refinement_20261003 (symlinked external disk).

Scoring and display
-------------------
The estimator exports raw metric world-from-body poses at original timestamps.
The official LaMAria evaluation functions derive one similarity transform from
the control points and apply it to the trajectory. The estimator does not use
GT or that transform. No public leaderboard submission is made here.

An evaluation Rerun reuses the exact saved CP_sim3 in lamaria_score/scores.json
for poses, rig, landmarks and point histories; it does not fit another transform.
Metric SE3 and dense Sim3 diagnostic fits are labeled separately and do not
change scored inputs. Score2D averages the official piecewise control-point
error contributions; missing control points contribute zero. Missing GT
samples remain in the full PoseRecall denominator, not in Score2D directly.

Common offline input
--------------------
The source run supplies 18,343 poses. Both native camera images are used at
every source pose: 36,686 images, with factory Fisheye624 calibration, a fixed
0.1375764865 m rig baseline, and native pixel coordinates converted by +0.5 px
to COLMAP's convention. SDK/COLMAP projection parity includes peripheral rays.
COLMAP SIFT extraction, matching and fixed-pose triangulation produce the same
immutable graph for every offline arm: 1,715,595 landmarks and 26,564,121
observations, including 310,108 cross-camera landmarks. No GT proposes matches.

Each arm starts independently from that graph, rather than the preceding arm:
  visual_rig: all supplied rig poses and landmarks, fixed intrinsics.
  vi_fixed:  the same visual graph, plus native IMU factors and inertial states.
  vi_calib:  the same VI graph, plus all 15 independent native parameters/camera.
The shared native focal is represented as fx=fy in COLMAP's 16-value storage.
VI includes every native IMU sample in every consecutive pose interval, with
exact interpolated boundaries, per-frame velocity/bias states and bias random
walks. Gravity direction is estimated, with its norm fixed at 9.81 m/s^2.

Calibration priors and limits are documented in README.txt. They stay centred
on the input factory calibration. Final projection failures and removed track
observations are explicitly counted. No features are dropped before the solve
for resource reasons. All original pose timestamps are retained and per-frame
visual/IMU support is exported.

The runner records input hashes, solver settings and command results. Profiling
and deliberately stopped attempts live under attempts/ or profiles/; they must
not be exported, scored or presented as complete refinements. A successful
30-step run is usable output, not automatically a convergence guarantee.

Online arm
-----------
The separate online_full_v2/v3 experiments replay the original native inputs
through the SLAM estimator, using ALIKED observations rather than the offline
SIFT graph. Periodic VI calibration uses only past keyframes. It jointly adjusts
poses, points, velocities, biases and all native camera intrinsic parameters,
with factory priors, per-step limits and held-out acceptance tests. Rig and
camera-to-IMU extrinsics remain fixed. Therefore the online and offline arms
are not a controlled comparison of only one variable.

v2 completed with Score2D 80.199161, 14/14 CPs within 1 m and 2999/2999 GT
associations; its CP scale was 1.01944346. It includes known transient rollback
jumps. v3 changes only the rollback snapshot boundary: preserve the IMU
prediction before visual relatching, so a rejected relatch cannot become the
fallback pose. Its full-sequence result was negative: Score2D 42.593277,
three independent maps, and 1,384/2,999 associated GT samples for the selected
largest map. The other 1,615 GT samples count as PoseRecall failures. Its surviving-map
error is a subset diagnostic and cannot be compared to full-route accuracy.
The rollback contract held in all 205 logged rejected initialized updates;
correct rollback alone did not produce robust continuity. Calibration/keyframe
scheduling also differed before the first failure, so this is not a perfectly
deterministic one-variable ablation. Preserve the successful v2 result.

Interpretation
---------------
Compare official score, coverage, metric scale, error distribution and solver
convergence together. A lower reprojection cost alone is not a trajectory win.
The online score improvement cannot yet be assigned exclusively to lens
calibration: recurring global VI optimization and lifecycle repairs changed
too. A future identical recurring-VI run with frozen intrinsics would isolate
that contribution. The requested offline vi_fixed/vi_calib pair does isolate
intrinsic freedom within their common SIFT graph.
