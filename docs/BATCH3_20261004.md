## Mechanisms

1. **Bias hold during visual starvation** (`patches/orbslam3_lamaria_biashold_20261004`,
   `new_files/include/LamariaBiasHold.h`). While the previous frame was coasting, RECENTLY_LOST or
   Baby-bridged, IMU biases are held at the last observed value: the Baby window solve pins and fixes
   every bias vertex; pose-only inertial optimisation and local inertial BA (keyframes inside a hold
   window) scale the random-walk edges by 1e4. Contracts: 9 checks (state machine, window membership,
   a 1e6 pull moves a free bias 0.018 rad/s but a held bias under 2e-5). Startup replay: 2346 frames,
   one map, zero hold windows, no crash. Log `[BIAS_HOLD] t= begin/end ... max_deviation=` (expect 0).
2. **Baby assist before loss, low weight** (`patches/orbslam3_lamaria_babyassist_20261004`,
   `new_files/include/LamariaBabyAssist.h`). Monitor: previous-frame inliers < 80 or local-map matches
   < 5 % of detected keypoints, five consecutive frames, IMU initialised, state OK, not coasting. Then one
   Baby window solve on a copy of the frame with ordinary-tracked frames fixed; verified landmarks
   (3+ views, 1 deg+ parallax, unassociated features, at most 150) enter the pose-only inertial
   optimisation as extra reprojection edges with information x 0.2 and Huber; inlier temporaries also
   enter the frame prior. Never MapPoints; cleared per frame. Calibration of the thresholds on healthy
   frames: inliers < 80 is the thinnest 5 % of Long and 9 % of Medium frames; a 10 % ratio fired on
   12-16 % of frames (rejected), 5 % fires on 2-3 %. Contracts: 10 checks. Log `[BABY_ASSIST] t=
   prev_inliers= matches= keypoints= solve= temporaries= used= chi2_temporaries= chi2_map= inliers_after=`.

## Finding from W4, and the proposed W8

In the W4 runaway coast (840-944 s) the Baby solver accepted 802 updates whose velocity change per
frame averaged 1.14 m/s (max 4.59; the acceptance bound was 5 m/s), at 62 inliers. In the surviving
W2 bridge (640-800 s) the same quantity averaged 0.036 m/s (max 1.31). A 1 m/s change in 50 ms is
20 m/s^2, impossible for a head-worn walker. Biases were held exactly (deviation 0), so bias wander is
not the driver; the Baby window solve itself drifts in velocity when the short tracks observe scale
weakly, and each accepted solve becomes the next window's anchor.

**W8 biashold_kin_medium (built and replay-tested, not launched):** `patches/orbslam3_lamaria_biashold_kin_20261004`
= bias hold + a kinematic acceptance gate in the Baby solve: a solve that changes the current frame's
velocity by more than 0.5 m/s is rejected (`kinematic_velocity_step`); the frame keeps its IMU
prediction. Expected: coasts stay near walking speed, the map relatches instead of splitting at the
10 s SOS limit. Risk: more rejected solves lengthen pure-IMU coasting; with held biases that drift is
about 1 m per 20 s instead of hundreds of metres.

**W9 babystereo_medium (built, contracts pass, not launched):** `patches/orbslam3_lamaria_babystereo_20261004`
= bias hold + kinematic gate + stereo-fused Baby tracks. The frame's own left-right fisheye matches give
a left and a right feature one track identity (`FuseStereoIds` in `LamariaBabyTracker.h`); the solver
admits one observation per camera per frame and may seed a temporary's depth from a same-frame
cross-camera pair, so the inter-camera baseline anchors the bridge scale metrically. Contracts: 17 Baby
solver checks (new: 26 cross-camera observations admitted with 8e-5 m depth error; a duplicate in the
same camera and frame still drops the track; the fusion rule inherits one-sided ids, shares a fresh id,
keeps two chains). Log `[BABY_STEREO] {pairs, inherited, fresh, kept_both}` every 200 frames.

## Results

Table: `/media/raghav/HardDrive1/MeckaAI/Raghavs_ORB-SLAM3/experiments/lamaria_batch3_20261004/table_r2/table.md` (18 rows)

- **Repeats.** Short 79.79 vs 81.39: Short is 80 +-1 for every config tried. Medium 78.59 -> 25.35 and
  74.41 -> 61.37 on byte-identical configs: Medium is decided by where the 640-944 s blackouts fall and
  how the Baby bridge re-anchors the map; single runs mean nothing there. Long 49.19 / 45.23 / 50.37 on
  the walks /10 config: spread about +-2.5.
- **Bias hold (W4, W5).** Mechanically exact (bias deviation 0 in every window) and neutral: the Medium
  runaway coast is driven by the Baby solve accepting about 1 m/s velocity jumps per frame, not by bias
  wander (W4 split anyway, 61.74); on Long the hold windows were 0.2-0.4 s and the score (50.37) is a
  third sample of the config. Kept as a safe component, not a lever.
- **Baby assist (W6, W7 and two repeats).** Long 46.90: active in the starved sections, no effect on the
  1700-1760 s step, within spread. Medium 82.88 / 56.79 / 61.33: with assist every Medium run kept one map
  (4 of 4, against 1 of 3 for rollback alone), so continuity is solved for this failure class; accuracy is
  not: one repeat drew a 17 s blackout whose bridge deformed the map, the other drifted slowly through
  500 s of thin tracking with no blackout at all, and the final-section loss (wearer looks down, no map
  points in view) ran away in two of four runs.
- **W8 kinematic gate: negative.** Rejecting a bridge solve turns deformation into a break (two splits).
- **W9 stereo-fused Baby tracks: neutral.** One map, mid-route bridges consistent (max 2.7 m to 1100 s),
  but only about 13 left-right pairs per frame, a uniform 0.8 m error level and the final-section runaway.
- **Where the Medium error now comes from.** (1) Slow scale drift through long thin-tracking sections
  (repeat 2), the same bias-absorbs-scale failure walks /10 cured on Long; assist + walks /10 has not been
  run together. (2) Long blackouts whose bridge re-anchors the map (repeat 3). (3) The final-section loss:
  plenty of keypoints, no map points in the new view, 35 s of coasting; survivable only by luck. Items 2
  and 3 need the map to keep extending into the new view while tracking thins: a thin-tracking keyframe
  policy (insert keyframes while the assist monitor is active and during a bridge, with the ordinary
  stereo seeding), not more bridging.

- **Knot hypothesis (read-only test).** For every control point on all three sequences the control-point
  error equals the trajectory's own error at the frames that see the tag (median ratio 0.96 Medium, 1.00
  Long and Short; hundreds of inlier detections per tag). Local accuracy at the tag frames cannot move the
  score; only tags as metric scale anchors (needs the physical tag size and our own corner detection) or
  as loop closures (one revisited tag on Medium, none on Long/Short) could.
- **Global fixed-camera VI refinement on finished runs.** Long 50.37 -> 49.08 (negative; earlier Long
  attempts gave +1.30 and +0.23: not a lever). Medium 82.88 -> 82.53 (18/18 control points within 1 m,
  PoseRecall@1m 85.2 -> 89.9 %, Score2D -0.36): the online solution already sits at the VI optimum; the
