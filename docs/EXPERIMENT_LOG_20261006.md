## 1. How to read this (10 lines)

1. Scoring: the official LaMAria toolkit, run locally. The estimator never sees ground truth. After the run, one Sim3 (scale + rotation + translation) is fitted on the control points (CPs, the AprilTags the wearer sweeps at), then every pose is compared to dense GT in the horizontal plane.
2. Score2D is the average of the per-CP piecewise error score; a CP that is not triangulated contributes zero. "CP <1 m" is how many CPs ended within one metre.
3. PoseRecall@5m is the share of all GT timestamps with a pose within 5 m; missing poses (before the first map, during a lost map) count as failures.
4. Only the largest map is scored: a split map loses every pose outside it. "Maps" is the number of maps in the atlas at the end.
5. "Coasting" frames are frames tracked on the IMU alone (state RECENTLY_LOST in the online csv); "lost" means no pose at all.
6. Sim3 scale is the fitted CP scale (estimate to GT); 1.000 means the map is metrically right, 1.08 means the map is 8 % too small.
7. Videos: Short = `sequence_1_19` (982 s), Medium = `sequence_2_11` (1256 s, the hard one), Long = `sequence_3_17` (1944 s), Long = `sequence_3_18` (1777 s, 24 CPs, first run in round 5). All are Meta's training recordings, scored one at a time.
8. References: Meta/Aria website Short 90.7, Medium 78.5, Long 70.9 (different test recordings, so not the same numbers). Mecka_Basalt best per training sequence: Short 1_19 67.1, Medium 2_11 39.0, Long 3_17 26.6, Long 3_18 21.5 (`/media/raghav/HardDrive1/Mecka/lamaria/bench_basalt/best_table.py`).
9. Every full run uses the same inputs: both native Fisheye624 cameras, cached ALIKED-derived binary descriptors, calibrated right IMU (or the fused left+right virtual IMU where stated), 4 CPUs, one Docker container per run.
10. Bias walks: "stock" = the Aria calibration's random-walk values (gyro 2.44e-4); "/10" = both walks divided by 10 (gyro 2.44e-5). Read from each run's `config/settings.yaml`.

Counts on disk: 93 case folders with a run under the five batch folders plus the init bench and knot clips (73 + 6 + 8 + 6), 65 of them with an official full-sequence score, plus 25 single runs from before the batches and 3 offline-VI refinements.

## 4. Round 2: Baby transfers, mechanism arms, dense features (2026-10-04, docs/BATCH_20261004.md, batches 1 and 2)

What it tried and taught: Baby SOS was transferred from Long to Short and Medium, then six mechanisms were tried on top of it: gyro prediction with a pre-init coast, a coast-age-widened search, IMU bias random walks /10 (and /30, and with calibrated 1x noise densities), dense ALIKED features, rejected-update rollback with a 3 m/s coast speed bound, and a pedestrian speed prior. Baby alone gave the first one-map Medium (71.40). Walks /10 was the one real lever: on Long it removed most of the late scale drift (43.73 to 49.19, max error 10.8 to 5.2 m). Rollback gave Medium 78.59 but, as round 3 showed, by luck. Every velocity/speed bound and the widened search were negative: a bound applied to one state at a time makes the inertial prediction inconsistent. A zero-keypoint frame crashed the estimator once; fixed (robust Frame). The decisive Medium failure is the 860-890 s open plaza, where one failed visual update leaves a bad velocity and pure-IMU coasting runs away.

## 9. Bugs and incidents

1. Keyframe-culling crash (four runs killed: w10a_knot_long at 721.0 s, w10a_r2_knot_medium_b at 250.8 s, l318_base_b at 657.0 s, l318_w13_report at 656.5 s; times are the last [LM_DIAG] frame before the SIGSEGV in run.log).
