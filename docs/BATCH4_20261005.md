## Mechanisms and their evidence

- **Knot regime** (`patches/orbslam3_lamaria_knot_20261005`, `new_files/include/LamariaKnot.h`): detector on
  10 s medians of gyro RMS (> 1.1 rad/s) and own speed (< 0.8 m/s) with 3 s hysteresis; keyframes at sweep
  reversals and every 15 deg of integrated rotation (at most 5 per second); Baby landmarks verified in 4+
  views promoted at knot keyframes (30 per keyframe, 300 per knot); local inertial BA window measured in
  time (5 s) with a whole-knot closure on the first keyframe after a knot; assist forced on. The first
  revision (10 deg, uncapped promotion) covered 15 of 17 Medium control-point windows with no false knot,
  but flooded the map (1,500-8,300 promotions per knot) and split the map twice in sample b.
- **Deterministic sequential mode** (`patches/orbslam3_lamaria_sequential_20261005`): after a keyframe
  insertion the tracker waits, at the start of the next frame and before taking the map-update mutex,
  until the mapper's queue is empty and it is idle (idle wait capped at 5 s; skipped while the mapper runs
  IMU initialisation; 60 s hard timeout). Two attempts that waited inside the frame or during
  initialisation deadlocked and were discarded.
- **Fused virtual IMU** (`pipeline/datasets/fuse_aria_imus.py`, data under
  `experiments/imu_fused_20261005/`): left IMU (1202-2, 800 Hz) extracted from the VRS with the production
  rectification; gyros averaged in the right frame; left accelerometer moved to the right IMU with the
  lever-arm terms and averaged. Validation: inter-IMU accelerometer residual 1.14 -> 0.14 m/s^2 on Long
  (wrong lever-arm sign: 2.24) and 1.17 -> 0.13 m/s^2 on Medium (2.31), gyro residual 0.014 rad/s on both. The left IMU has no published noise
  model; the right IMU's settings are kept.

## Results

- **Assist + IMU walks /10 (W11 b): 83.92, 18 of 18 control points within 1 m, max error 1.3 m anywhere.**
  The two positive mechanisms together: assist keeps tracking alive through thin sections (17 Baby entries
  against 18 and 28 in its own repeats and 37-69 in the batch-3 assist and knot Medium runs), the tighter bias walks stop the slow scale creep. First Medium run with
  every control point inside a metre. Rerun under `w11_assist_walk10_medium_b/evaluation_baby_20261005/`.
- **Knot regime (W10a-d, two revisions): negative.** The detector holds up (15 of 17 Medium control-point
  windows recognised in each run, no false knots) but acting inside knots hurt: knot keyframes at 3-5 per
  second plus Baby promotions (even capped) and assist on every knot frame produced 916-1,773 coasting
  frames per Medium run (222 on Long) against 103-106 for the assist baseline, deformed maps, splits, and
  two identical crashes in the mapper's keyframe culling (SetBadFlag -> ChangeParent -> AddChild on an
  invalid KeyFrame pointer) while the tracker was in the descriptor matcher. The rev-2 "matcher guard"
  therefore guarded the wrong thread; the real fault is a dangling keyframe pointer in the spanning tree
  under the knot keyframe rate. The regime is dropped; the detector stays as a diagnostic. What it taught: more keyframes and more landmarks during fast sweeps overload local mapping;
  the lever inside knots is fewer, better keyframes, not more.

## W11 on Medium: three samples, three outcomes

Same binary, same config (settings sha 8d640bc7, byte-identical), same machine: a 69.58 (13/18 within 1 m),
b 83.92 (18/18), c 49.11 (2/18). The spread is not noise in the control points; it is one event that happens
in c and not in a or b. What precedes it happens in all three:

- 818-822 s: online calibration step 13 runs in the mapper (trial rejected, `parameter_bound_cam0`) and the
  Baby window is reset by `map_update` 8 times in 4 s while the mapper rewrites the map. a and b see the same
  step and even more resets (19 and 23 in the same window) and track through it.
- 821.4 s: ordinary tracking drops to 1 inlier ("Fail to track local map!"), coasting engages with a runaway
  propagated speed (6.3 m/s, clamped to 3 m/s by the guard), place recognition finds nothing (0 BoW candidates).
- The Baby assist window re-latches within 0.2 s (accepted solves with 121-256 inliers), but the state it
  re-latches to is already wrong: at 845 s tracking is lost again for 29 s (494 frames, all RELATCH attempts
  fail) and the run only recovers at 874 s. In a the same seconds track with 66-464 inliers; in b with 19-273 (6 failed frames, no loss).
- Result: the whole map is deformed (scale 0.980, 16/18 control points between 1.0 and 5.1 m), not a local break.

Reading: the three runs behave alike up to the moment ordinary tracking collapses to one inlier at 821.4 s
in c; what makes c collapse where a and b hold is not identified in the logs. The method is only as
reproducible as the tracker/mapper race around the calibration and map updates. That race is what W12 (sequential mode) was built to remove; see its verdict below.

Online calibration cost (not previously measured): each calibration step on Medium takes minutes to tens of
minutes of wall clock near the end of the sequence (the 19th step in c held tracking for 21-24 min, bounded
from the runner heartbeats), against under 2 min per step on Short and Long at the same observation count
(30-90 s per step in the W14a Long b and W11 Short b container logs read at 05:20; Short/Long heartbeats never
idle longer than 124 s). In W12 sequential mode the steps grow from 3 min at 567 s to 48 min at 868 s (step 14
of b, container log 23:00:45-23:48:25 UTC) and to over 2 h by step 19. This is why Medium a and b took about
2 h (c 64 min, alone on the machine for most of it) and the W12 pair runs into the afternoon.
