## Read-only decomposition first (E41)

Before any run, the Baby Long 43.73 output was decomposed read-only
(`pipeline/eval/decompose_lamaria_errors.py`, Stages 0-2, 15 synthetic contracts pass,
official per-timestamp errors reproduced to 7e-13 m):

- Missing start = 71 frames before any map + 148 frames of a visual-only first map that lost
  tracking in a 0.5 s blur/detector-dropout burst at 163.25 s while the 12 s IMU gate was still
  open, + 4 re-init frames.
- End drift is local SCALE error born in the live tracking (online and post-BA stretch agree
  within 0.6 %), not heading and not an alignment artefact: 15 m-window displacement ratio
  0.85-0.91 after 1800 s versus 0.97-1.02 mid-route.
- The start error is mostly real deformation: a 1.1 deg heading error over 282 m of path
  explains the north component; a ~1 % local scale excess explains the east offset.
- Tilt: the estimator's gravity is within 0.3 deg of GT vertical from orientations; the 4.3 deg
  tilt belongs to the 27-control-point Sim3 (13 height CPs, all late). The viewer adds nothing.

## Verdict per sequence

- **Short:** 81.39 (Baby + walks /10) versus 80.20 frozen; 14/14 CPs within 1 m. Within the ±1 run spread,
  so not a proven lever on Short, but nothing regressed.
- **Medium:** two one-map candidates. Rejected-update rollback alone scores **78.59** (17/18 CPs within
  1 m, level with Meta 78.5) but the last 35 s coast runs off (last CP 250 m). Rollback + walks /10 scores
  74.41 with the tail tamed (max error 2.1 m anywhere, PoseRecall@5m 99.9 %) at the cost of 1.5 m drift
  over the final 250 s. Leaderboard pick: 78.59. Robustness pick: 74.41.
- **Long:** walks /10 is the lever (49.19, and 45.23 on a repeat with identical startup; 50.04 with 1x
  densities, 49.42 with offline VI) against Baby 43.73 and historical 49.88; /30 over-constrains mid-route. The spread between otherwise similar Long
  runs (38.5-50.0) is set by the startup lottery: early CPs 4-8 m, worst when three pre-init resets delay
  the retained map to 166.0 s. Meta 70.9 stays far; the next lever is initialisation repeatability.

## What each mechanism did

1. **Baby SOS on Medium.** Bridged the 770-778 s break of frozen v2 and every later blackout; one
   map, 18/18 CPs, 91 % of poses within 1 m before 1160 s. From 1160 s the wearer looks at the
   ground while walking through a crowd (cam0: cobblestones and feet; cam1: pedestrians);
   visual updates confirm whatever the IMU predicts and the speed estimate rises 1.0 -> 3.6 m/s,
   then the final 30 s coast on the IMU and the tail diverges (last CP 27 m off).
2. **Pre-init coast (gyropred).** Fired exactly as designed on the Long startup profile (map kept,
   IMU initialised 8 s earlier) and in both full runs, but the kept startup map gave a worse
   metric initialisation and the whole route is deformed on both sequences. Dropped.
3. **Coast-age-widened search.** During a 57 s IMU coast the wider window admitted wrong
   relatches and destroyed the first map (max error 128 m). Dropped.
4. **IMU bias random walks /10.** On Long: 43.73 -> 49.19 with one map, median error 1.90 -> 1.40 m,
   maximum 10.8 -> 5.2 m; the late scale drift that the decomposition attributed to live tracking is
   largely gone. The Aria calibration's raw bias random walks (gyro 10x, accel 2x EuRoC-class values)
   let biases absorb scale under weak vision. On Medium the retained-map accuracy is unchanged and the
   run split at 882 s in the marginal 860-890 s section (see below). Offline fixed-camera VI on the
   Long result adds only +0.23 (49.42).
5. **Robust Frame (fix).** A frame whose keypoints are all rejected crashed the estimator
   (Frame constructors returned before allocating the IMU mutex). Fixed in
   `patches/orbslam3_lamaria_babyfeats_rf_20261004`; all arms after it use that tree.
8. **Rejected-update rollback (`Tracking.coastRestore: 1`) + 3 m/s coast guard.** On Medium the run
   never coasted at 860-890 s or 1040 s and the end-of-route scale creep did not appear (1.6-1.7 m/s
   against GT 1.5-1.6): 78.59, 17/18 CPs. The 3 m/s guard fired only in the final coast and is twice
   walking speed, so it cannot help. On Long both rollback runs had bad starts (three pre-init resets)
   and one had a 103 m tail; the tail arm tests whether bounded Baby/coast velocities remove that.
7. **Pedestrian speed-bound prior** (`patches/orbslam3_lamaria_speedprior_20261004`,
   `new_files/include/LamariaSpeedPrior.h`). Read-only GT check (evaluation data only): the 1 s-window
   speed never exceeds 1.84 m/s on Short/Medium/Long (p99.9 1.74-1.83; standstills under 0.15 m/s are
   2-3 % of the time and never longer than 3 s, so a zero-velocity update would rarely fire). Baby Medium
   estimated 2.5-3.0 m/s during 1170-1215 s (GT 1.5-1.6) and 2.6-5.1 m/s during the final SOS/coast
   1220-1256 s (GT 0.5-1.3); coastguard_long coasted at 3-7 m/s after 1820 s. One-sided soft prior
   e = max(0, |v| - 2.2 m/s), sigma 0.01 m/s, on body-velocity vertices in pose-only inertial
   optimisation, local inertial BA and the Baby solver; IMU propagation clamped to the same bound.
   Silent below the bound (startup plumbing run: 2347 frames, 0 firings, 1 map). Both variants were
  negative on Medium (19.98 all stages; 16.17 Baby + propagation only): a velocity bound applied to one
  state at a time makes the inertial prediction inconsistent and tracking coasts. Dropped. Contract: 11 checks
   (Jacobian vs finite differences, bound reached along the velocity direction, untouched below the
   bound, equal-information compromise at 2.6 m/s, propagation clamp).
6. **Dense ALIKED caches** (`pipeline/datasets/extract_aliked_dense.py`, same binarization):
   never sparser than the old caches; 2-3x more keypoints where the old runs starved (Medium
   770-778 s: 988 -> 2756; Long after 1550 s: 909 -> 2016). Caches:
   `/media/raghav/HardDrive1/MeckaAI/Raghavs_ORB-SLAM3/experiments/inputs_aliked_dense_20261004/`.

## The failure that decides Medium continuity

The 860-890 s section (open plaza, distant buildings, plain ground) is marginal. Runs that lose
tracking there show the inertial state going bad first: one failed visual update leaves 6.7 m/s
in the frame (coastRestore is 0 in every frozen config), pure IMU coasting then accelerates to
41 m/s within 14 s, landmarks project nowhere, Baby cannot triangulate, the 10 s SOS limit
expires and the map splits. The run that passed never coasted there (speed 0.4-1.6 m/s).
The coastguard arm tests the two direct remedies.
